Studio Konten Pribadi

Studio Konten Pribadi is a personal, single-user tool used by its owner to prepare educational videos and to upload and schedule them on the owner's own YouTube channels.

What the app does

  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channels as private, scheduled videos.
  • Updates the title, description, thumbnail and publishing schedule of the owner's own videos.
  • Reads public search results and the owner's own channel statistics to plan new topics.

The app is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer.
